Need to analyze your Google Ads campaign performance across multiple accounts? This template automatically extracts comprehensive campaign data and metrics from Google Ads, giving you a complete view of your advertising performance. Perfect for marketing teams and agencies managing multiple campaigns.

Get started in minutes: Simply authenticate your Google Ads account, select the accounts you want to analyze, choose your preferred date range (e.g., last week), and get immediate access to key metrics like impressions, clicks, costs, and engagement rates for all your campaigns.
