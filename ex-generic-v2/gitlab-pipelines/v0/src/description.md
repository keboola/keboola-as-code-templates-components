Need to analyze your CI/CD pipeline performance? This template extracts comprehensive pipeline data from GitLab, giving you deep insights into your development workflow. Perfect for DevOps teams looking to optimize their CI/CD processes and track deployment metrics.

Get started in minutes: Simply provide your GitLab API token and the template will automatically collect data about your pipelines, jobs, and cross-project relationships from the last 3 days. You'll see detailed execution times, success rates, and pipeline relationships across your projects.
