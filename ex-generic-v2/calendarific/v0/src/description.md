# Calendarific Holidays Data Source

Need to keep track of holidays across multiple countries for your business planning? This template automatically retrieves official national holidays for the Czech Republic, Canada, United States, and Great Britain, helping you maintain an accurate holiday calendar for international operations.

Get started in minutes: Simply provide your Calendarific API key and enter "2024" as the target year. The template will create four tables with holiday dates and names - perfect for checking business days, planning international campaigns, or scheduling global team meetings. For example, you'll see entries like "Christmas Day" on "2024-12-25" across different countries, making it easy to coordinate international activities.
