Need to analyze your incident management data and on-call schedules? This template extracts comprehensive operational data from Opsgenie, giving you insights into alert patterns, team performance, and on-call coverage.

Get started in minutes: Simply provide your Opsgenie API token and the template will automatically extract your alerts from the past 3 days, complete team roster, user details, and on-call schedules for the past 12 months. Perfect for creating incident response dashboards or analyzing team workload distribution.
