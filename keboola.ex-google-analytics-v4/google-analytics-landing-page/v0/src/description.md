Need to understand how visitors interact with different entry points of your website? This template extracts comprehensive landing page engagement metrics from Google Analytics 4, helping you identify your most effective entry pages and optimize the visitor journey.

Get started in minutes: Simply connect your GA4 account, select the properties you want to analyze, and set your preferred date range. The template will extract key metrics like user counts, session duration, and engagement rates per landing page, segmented by device category.
