Need to analyze how users interact with your website or app? This template extracts comprehensive event engagement data from Google Analytics 4, helping you understand user behavior, track revenue, and identify your most important events.

Get started in minutes: Simply connect your GA4 account, select your preferred date range (e.g., last month), and get immediate insights into event performance, user engagement, and revenue metrics. Perfect for marketing analysts and product managers who need to measure user interactions and campaign effectiveness.
